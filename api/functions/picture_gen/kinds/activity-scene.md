Kind: activity scene (activity-scene). A small picture that fills its square, shown in a learning activity at about
128 px: weather, a time of day, a place.

- Canvas: 256 x 256. The scene fills the whole square: a rounded-square background shape (rect x 24, y 24, 208 x 208,
  rx 40, plum outline) holds the scene, and everything stays inside it.
- Anchor: the centre (128,128).
- Background: allowed and expected inside the rounded square (sky, ground, water, night). Outside the rounded square
  the corners stay transparent.
- Outline: 7 for the frame and the main shapes, inner lines 4, fine detail 3.
- Keep it simple: one main idea and at most three or four supporting shapes, readable at 128 px.
- Safety: friendly, never frightening, for ages 5-7; no weapons, blood or gore; no letters, numbers or letter-like shapes.
