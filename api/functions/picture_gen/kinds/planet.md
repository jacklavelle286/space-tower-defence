Kind: planet (planet). A whole planet or moon seen from space, for the campaign map, scaled into 256 x 256.

- Canvas: 256 x 256. One round disc, centre (128,136), r 98, leaving headroom at the top for anything
  that stands on it (domes, an antenna).
- Anchor: the centre (128,128).
- Inside the disc: soft patches and a few round craters, kept inside with a clipPath; a cyan rim light on the
  top-left edge; one shade crescent on the bottom-right.
- Background: none. Real transparency; no stars or space behind it.
- Outline: 7 (the 512 example uses 12; at 256 it is 7).
- The first example, obj-planet.svg, is a small counting planet: copy only its shading and how its ring passes behind
  and in front of the disc, not its size or position (the disc here is centre (128,136), r 98).
- The second example, planet-luna-1.svg, is drawn on a 512 canvas: copy its construction and halve its numbers.
  Ignore the numbers in its top comment.
- Safety: friendly, never frightening, for ages 5-7; no weapons, blood or gore; no letters, numbers or letter-like shapes.
